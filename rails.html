Money, not a pitch

Three rails — people choose

Best shape, from the X thread: Kaspa · PoC KUSD · Tether-like stables. Label them. Do not weld them. Miner fee is always KAS.

A dApp, a till, and an HTTP 402 endpoint all spend some unit. That unit is a political surface. If it can be frozen by an issuer, the app can be frozen. If you hide a freeze inside a Kaspa wrapper and call it native, you lied. Three labelled rails keep the choice honest.

RailKindFreezeUse for
KAS native PoW No Dapp unit if you can bear volatility. Always miner fee. Always the x402 asset if you bind one.
kUSD PoC / reserved-native No issuer key in the design Candidate stable unit. Not live money. Not x402.
USDT-like guest IOU Yes. Issuer addBlackList / destroyBlackFunds Merchant dollar inventory, labelled. Never gas. Never miner fee.

Why three, not one

Kaspa (native KAS) is proof-of-work cash on L1. Same test Bitcoin has held since 2009: there is no issuer function that zeros a UTXO you do not have the keys to. Crescendo made it fast (10 blocks per second). Toccata made it programmable (covenants). Neither added a blacklist key. Volatility is the cost of that property.

PoC KUSD is the attempt to quote a dollar without importing Tether’s freeze switch — typically a KAS-backed covenant (BitCoffee is one TN10 protocol). No issuer key in the design is the point. Peg unproven. Not shops. Not the x402 asset. Calling a reserved chair “a dollar” is welding.

Tether-like stables (USDT, USDC, and bridges that honour them) are IOUs. Self-custody removes the exchange. It does not remove the issuer. Bridging them onto Kaspa does not make GHOSTDAG the issuer. Useful as labelled guest inventory. Forbidden as gas, miner fee, or the unit the dApp dies without.

Do not weld them

Welding is when one name covers two objects: “Kaspa dollar” for Tether-on-Kaspa, or “native stable” for an empty till seat, or “x402” for a coffee POS. People then cannot tell which freeze rule they accepted. Label the rail on the ticket. If the guest can be frozen, say so on the ticket.

Miner fee is always KAS

Block space is paid to miners in the coin they mine. If the fee asset is Tether, miners sit under a freeze-capable issuer. That is a different system. Quote EUR or USD on a keypad if a human wants a price. Settlement and the fee are separate objects. Pick a rail for settlement. The fee stays KAS even on a dollar rail.

Constitution

  1. Consensus / order = Kaspa L1. USDT does not vote in GHOSTDAG.
  2. Miner fee = KAS. Sender pays.
  3. Unit of account on a keypad = EUR or USD.
  4. Settlement is a separate object. Pick a rail.
