Private DevNet

1985

Experimental research on this desk. It is a place to look at one private Kaspa DevNet. It is not a product. It is not a stablecoin. It is not Kaspa core.

The 1984 tab is unchanged. The town, the rails, and Testnet 10 stay there. This page has this DevNet only.

A funded desk wallet holds Kaspa that this DevNet already mined. You can send that Kaspa to another kaspadev address. The miner fee is that same Kaspa. STP is a lab face written into the coinbase. The script ends in OP_FALSE, so the sompi stays in the UTXO set and does not pay as Kaspa. You cannot send STP. The face is not a balance. It is the number stored next to the locked sompi.

Intentions are good. The thought process is questionable.
