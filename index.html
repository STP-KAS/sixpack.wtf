Kaspa Explained STP

-Bitcoin started as proof of work: scarce money and ownership that does not depend on who already holds the coins.

-Proof of stake replaced work with capital. That is a different system.

-Kaspa kept Bitcoin’s proof of work and upgraded it: 10 blocks per second on average, now programmable.

Grok Bot

STP-KAS/Xai.Kaspa.node — run a Kaspa archival node inside the Grok Bot Linux sandbox. Not your PC. Not your phone. You chat; the node lives on xAI’s Linux box.

What: paste START.md into a bot named kaspa bot. It runs rusty-kaspa archival, keepalive, a tip ticker, news, Discord-first help, a no-price explainer, and an “am I live?” card. Why: so someone with Grok Bot can run a real node without a VPS or a melted laptop. This sandbox’s live fleet: 1 mainnet node, 1 Testnet-10 node, 150 Testnet-10 miners (tn10 bot). Experimental.
