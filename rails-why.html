Rails

Four ways to pay sit on the 1984 square. They are not the same object. This page says what each one is and why it is here. The longer essay is 1984 raw idea. The square is 1984. This is a classroom. It is not Kaspa core.

tKAS

What. tKAS is Testnet-10 KAS, the coin that moves.

Why. It is the mining coin. The miner fee is always KAS, at 87 times the standard Testnet 10 rate, and it is extra. That fee is not taken from a tag and it is not taken from an OLAF note.

How. A funded address spends tKAS at a counter, or takes it to the bank. The bank locks it into POCencept or KUSDT, or shields it into OLAF. A tKAS payment that has a txid is the Testnet 10 transaction.

POCencept

What. POCencept is a public tag. A SquarePeg covenant locks tKAS, and the tag is the keypad amount on that lock. The face is cents on this square. It is not a dollar.

Why. The lock can be redeemed. Redeem returns the locked tKAS in proportion to the tag that remains. A later quote does not reprice the lock. Nobody freezes POCencept.

How. Swap tKAS for POCencept at the bank. A shop spend sends that share of tKAS to the reserve, and the tag does not continue. An exchange with KUSDT flips the tag and keeps the sompi. Kachat charges 0.01 POCencept. A Layer-Kaspa offer can be paid in POCencept.

KUSDT

What. KUSDT is the other public tag. It locks tKAS the same way POCencept does. It is not a dollar, and it is not Tether.

Why. The issuer can freeze the tag. That is the classroom for a freeze. A freeze blocks KUSDT spending and swaps. The tKAS already locked on that tag can still be redeemed. POCencept in the same moment still moves. The miner fee is not taken from the lock.

How. Swap tKAS for KUSDT at the bank. Redeem, exchange, and a shop spend follow the same SquarePeg path as POCencept. The freeze is KUSDT only, and the issuer signs it.

OLAF

What. OLAF is a shielded dollar note. 100 units are 1.00 OLAF, one dollar of tKAS at the quote when that note was made. A later quote does not reprice that note.

Why. POCencept and KUSDT are numbers on the public ledger. OLAF is a note only this browser can open. The public book stores a commitment, a ciphertext, and a nullifier when the note is spent. It does not store the owner or the amount. The key stays in this browser. A lost key cannot be reset by the till.

How. Shield tKAS at the bank. The coins stay in the square reserve as backing. A shop, the cinema, a flight, a gift card, SI stream, and the roadster can spend the note. The price of that purchase is public. The change note stays sealed. A private send nullifies the old note and writes a new one. The pool total stays the same.

What it does not do. You cannot redeem OLAF for tKAS. Spending a note burns that dollar. There is no chain payout. The old public 1,000,000.00 supply was deleted. New OLAF exists only as a note backed by tKAS paid in after that. OLAF does not pass through SquarePeg. The mint desk does not mint it. Hunt Hall does not take it. This is not a zero-knowledge proof and not a settlement covenant. The Kaspa Privacy Initiative is separate research. This square does not run that Based App proof. This is not Kaspa core. KCC-20 is Last Call, not Final.

Where they are taken

A counter, the cinema ticket, the snacks, a hop, the gift cards, SI stream, and the roadster take tKAS, POCencept, KUSDT, or OLAF.

Hunt Hall takes tKAS, POCencept, and KUSDT. It does not take OLAF.

Kachat is 0.01 POCencept. A Layer-Kaspa offer is POCencept or KUSDT. The miner fee on every path is KAS.

Why they stay apart

tKAS is the coin. POCencept is a public tag you can redeem, with no freeze. KUSDT is that same kind of tag, with a freeze. OLAF is a private dollar note, and redeem does not pay the tKAS back.

Do not weld them. A promise on this square does not buy a car, an SI service, a game, or a rental. The spend gate stays shut. The platform essay is 1984 raw idea.
