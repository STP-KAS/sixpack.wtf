Who is reading comes first. Models you can press, not a lecture. Overlay of Parker’s education. Cite parker2017code/kaspa-explained. Public site: kaspaexplained.com. This page is not that site.

Door 1 · Beginner

Public cash. You hold keys.

A bank can freeze an account. Here the list is public. You sign a payment. Miners spend energy so rewriting history is expensive. That is proof of work. Proof of stake replaced work with a vote of who already holds the coin. Kaspa did not take that turn.

  • Keys prove a payment is yours. Lose them, the coins are gone. Share a recovery phrase, you gave them away.
  • Wallet holds keys. It is not a bank. An exchange balance is a company IOU.
  • Send → include → accept. The recipient chooses how long to wait. Sending is not settling.
  • Kaspa keeps parallel honest blocks and orders them (blockDAG). About ten blocks per second. That is not instant finality.
  • Never paste a seed. Testnet-10 tKAS is not mainnet KAS. Price is not a source.

Model · a payment

You → Network → Them

Accepted in the current history. The recipient may still wait before treating it as settled.

Inclusion, acceptance, and a shop’s wait are three different things. There is no undo.

Door 2 · Intermediate

Bitcoin throws extra honest blocks away. Kaspa keeps them and orders them.

Still proof of work. Still UTXO. Not Ethereum. Not an L2. A DAG is not a free pass to spend twice.

  • Bitcoin: one chain, extras orphaned. Kaspa: concurrent PoW, ordered by GHOSTDAG. Double-spend is checked after order.
  • Inclusion, acceptance, and the recipient’s wait are three facts. Same as Bitcoin, faster blocks.
  • Covenants are spending rules on outputs. Solidity does not run on Kaspa L1.
  • Live: GHOSTDAG, 10 BPS, Toccata. Compiler tag: SilverScript v1.0.0 (apps are separate). Prototype: Argent. Research: vProgs. Proposed: DAGKnight.
  • A tweet is not a KIP. 100 BPS is research.

Model · one output, two attempts

10 KAS, one output. Both payments name it. After order, only one can spend it.

Alice’s payment consumes the output. Bob’s attempt is already spent. Keeping both blocks does not make both payments valid.

Door 3 · Advanced

A live rule is not an app.

Toccata is live. SilverScript v1.0.0 is a compiler tag, not production dApps. vProgs are research. DAGKnight is Proposed. Do not mix those sentences.

  • Do not claim: 100 BPS live, instant irreversible coffee, Ethereum-class native contracts, KCC-20 adopted, vProgs live, Toccata “coming soon.”
  • Build on Testnet-10 (kaspatest:). Quote DAA and subsidy from status; they age.
  • A covenant can lock, wait, cap, name a destination. It is not a bank, a court, or an AMM.

Door 5 · Builder

Covenants if the job needs a spend rule. Kaspa Call is not a KIP.

Toccata is live. A coin can carry rules. SilverScript v1.0.0 is the compiler tag. Kaspa Calls for Conventions (KCC) are how wallets and apps might agree on bytes. They do not change consensus. All KCCs are still Draft.

  • Need a vault, escrow, receipt, native asset? L1 covenants. Guide: docs.kaspa.org/toccata.
  • See coins that already carry rules: kascov.io.
  • KIP 16/17/20/21 Active. KCC-20 Draft (supply-split issue open). Do not ship “KCC-20 token” as law.
  • KaspaZ is a different chain. Not this.
