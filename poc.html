PoC revisited

15 Sep 2026. Not in the main bar — the map is home. Desk kUSD chair vs BitCoffee protocol. GitHub: STP-KAS/poc-revisited · kaspa-dapps.

Why the desk PoC exists. Centralised stables can be frozen and destroyed by the issuer. Bitcoin has no such key. Kaspa inherits that on native KAS. Bridging Tether onto Kaspa is useful liquidity. It is not a replacement for a rail that cannot be switched off.

What changed. BitCoffee0 published a TN10 KUSD that is overcollateralized with KAS, oracle-free by construction (fixed Module price), and actually accepted by Testnet 10. The chair is no longer the only object named kUSD.

What Grok did, why, sources

Did: built the dual-rail lesson (native vs Tether), reserved a kUSD name so the till would not only speak USDT, refused kUSD as x402 asset, kept PegLab as the thing that depegs, bound kaspa-x402 to native KAS.

Why: dapps sequenced on Kaspa need stable operating costs without importing a king into the unit. Waiting for Tether is waiting for a freeze switch.

Sources: Parker kaspa-explained; Ishum; PegLab; Gramlane; sixpack.wtf kaspa-x402 pass; Tether blacklist public record in WHY-NOT-ONLY-TETHER.md; Sutton on partitioned app state; Luke kaspa-x402.
